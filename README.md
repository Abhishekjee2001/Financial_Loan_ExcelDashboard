# Financial_Loan_ExcelDashboard

## Financial Loan Analysis Dashboard## 📌 Project Overview
This repository hosts an interactive Financial Loan Analysis Dashboard designed to monitor, track, and optimize a lending institution's portfolio performance. By consolidating key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics, this dashboard provides critical insights into total loan distribution, repayment metrics, borrower risk profiles, and historical trend trajectories.
The primary goal is to help risk management and financial operations teams maximize the loan recovery rate while proactively minimizing non-performing assets (bad debt).
## 📊 Business Key Metrics (KPIs)

* Total Applications: 37,143 loans processed.
* Total Approved Loan Amount: ₹41,89,66,300.00 distributed to borrowers.
* Total Recovered Loan Amount: ₹45,56,62,103.00 collected.
* Bad Debt Amount: ₹6,22,48,700.00 recognized as non-performing or defaulted loans.
* Average Defaulter Rate: 13.64% portfolio risk exposure.
* Recovered Loan Rate: 83.49% successful collection efficiency.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Used

* BI Platform: Microsoft Excel (Advanced Pivot Tables, Interactive Slicers, Dashboard Designing)
* Data Transformation: Power Query (ETL processing, currency formatting, data cleaning)
* Visualizations Engine: Excel Charts (Line charts, Column charts, Area charts)
* Core Metrics Analytics: Advanced Formulas, Ratio Analyses, Portfolio Risk Segmentation

## 📉 Dashboard Architecture & Insights## 1. High-Level KPI Banners

* Placed prominently at the top of the interface to capture real-time financial health, cash inflows versus outflows, and bad debt impact at a glance.

## 2. Loan Intent & Purpose Segmentation

* Total Applications & Volume By Purpose: Breaks down loan distributions by categories (e.g., Debt Consolidation, Credit Cards, Home Improvements) to uncover shifting market demands.

## 3. Chronological Financial Trends

* Approved Loan Trend By Month: A continuous upward-sloping line chart mapping monthly loan volumes to evaluate institution growth and compound interest expansion across the financial year.

## 4. Risk Profile & Credit Grading Analysis

* Total Received Loan Payment By Grade: Evaluates how much cash is successfully retrieved across internal risk tiers (Grades A through G).
* Avg Interest Rate & Total Loan Amount By Sub-Grade: Visualizes pricing risk distribution, highlighting how interest rates scale dynamically against lower borrower sub-grades.

## 5. Demographic & Operational Breakdown

* Employment Tiers: Correlates loan volumes against employment lengths (<1 Year, 5 Years, 10+ Years) to test stability baselines.
* Income Category Filters: Groups risk metrics across High, Medium, and Low-income brackets.
* Geographic Map Lines: Distributes fully paid loans and active default spikes across regional state lines.

------------------------------
## 🚀 Interactive Features

* Dynamic Slicers: Filter the entire canvas instantaneously by Grade, Employment Category, Issue Date (Time-blocks), and State Regions.
* Cross-Filtering: Selecting a specific category dynamically updates all KPI metrics across the dashboard.
